Privacy Policy — Bot by Jaseem

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Bot by Jaseem ("the app") is a desktop application that helps creators render short videos and publish them to their own YouTube, Facebook and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information the app handles

  • Account authorization tokens. When you connect an account (YouTube / Google, GitHub, Facebook, or TikTok), the platform issues an access token. The token is stored only on your own computer, in the app's data folder, and is used solely to perform the publishing actions you configure.
  • Your content and settings. The text, images, videos, music and schedules you choose stay on your computer. If you enable the app's optional cloud mode, copies of your settings and media are stored in a private repository in your own GitHub account so the schedule can run while your PC is off, and the relevant platform token is stored there as an encrypted secret managed by GitHub.
  • No collection by us. The app does not send your personal information, your content, or your tokens to the developer or to any analytics or advertising service. The only network requests the app makes are to the platforms you connected (for example YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, GitHub) to carry out your instructions, and a licence-status check that sends only a one-way hashed device identifier and the licence code you entered.

Platform APIs

The app uses official APIs: the YouTube API Services, the TikTok Content Posting API (Login Kit), the Facebook Graph API, and the GitHub API. Use of each platform is also governed by that platform's own privacy policy and terms. The app requests only the permissions needed to publish the videos you configure (for TikTok: user.info.basic and video.publish).

Your choices

  • You can disconnect any account inside the app at any time, which deletes the stored token from your computer.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time from the platform's own account settings (for example your Google, TikTok, Facebook or GitHub security pages).
  • The app's "Clear Data" function permanently deletes all of its stored settings and tokens from your computer.

Children

The app is a publishing tool intended for account holders; it is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Bot by Jaseem

A desktop app that helps creators prepare and publish scheduled short videos to their own channels and accounts.

What it does

Bot by Jaseem is a Windows desktop application for content creators. A creator connects their own accounts, chooses their own text, images, videos and music, and sets a daily schedule. The app then renders a vertical short video and publishes it to the creator's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page, or TikTok account using each platform's official API and the permissions the creator grants.

Features

  • Scheduled daily uploads at times the creator chooses
  • Custom text, branding, backgrounds, music and animations
  • Optional cloud mode: publishing continues on schedule even when the PC is off
  • Each user signs in with their own accounts — the app never uses anyone else's

Accounts and permissions

The app only accesses an account after the account owner signs in and authorizes it, and it only performs the actions the owner configured (publishing their videos). Authorization can be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings.
